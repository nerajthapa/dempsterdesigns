Six Decades of Crafting Visual Excellence

For over 60 years, Dempster Designs has led the signage and design industry by blending evolving technology with timeless visual communication principles. Our reputation is built on quality craftsmanship and a deep respect for every client's vision, investment, and brand.

We deliver everything from brand identity and promotional materials to event and campaign design with precision and professionalism. Using durable materials and expert installation, we ensure every piece achieves maximum impact.

Our strength lies in integrating visual, print, and audio elements to create cohesive, engaging brand experiences. From custom illustrations and photography to music and radio support, we provide end-to-end creative solutions-helping businesses communicate clearly, stand out, and grow.
